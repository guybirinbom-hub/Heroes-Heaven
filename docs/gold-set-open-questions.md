# Gold set — the questions I could not answer

Your ten answers settled far more than ten feats. Applying the principles to the other 33 left a set of
open calls, and they **cluster**: 33 feats raise **14 distinct decisions**, several of which recur.

Each decision below names the feats it settles and, where I could measure it, **how many feats in the
whole corpus it decides**. That is the number worth weighing — some of these are one-off, and some set
precedent for a hundred records.

Answer by number: *"Q3 — option (b)"*. Feat numbers in brackets refer to the review document.

---

## The big ones — each sets precedent well beyond this set

### Q1 · A durationed ally-facing effect: static marker, or display-only mode?
**Decides ~100 feats.** Feats: 4, 5, 20, 28, 34.

Your three sources point different ways, and I could not choose between them:

- Your **#1 Resilient Touch** answer put an indication in Lay on Hands' description and said nothing
  about a mode. That effect ran on the **ally's** clock — "until the end of *their* next turn" — which
  the sheet cannot see.
- Your **#3 Healing Sanctuary** answer ordered a **mode** on the anchor ability, with the modifying
  feat reflected in its text and no numbers.
- **Ruling F case 2** says an ally-facing effect that runs for a limited time gives the activator a
  display-only mode so they can see it running.

Fortunate Blow is the sharp case: its window is *"before the start of **your** next turn"* — your own
clock, which a mode *could* show.

**(a)** marker on the anchor record only · **(b)** display-only mode on the anchor · **(c)** mode only
when the duration runs on *your* clock, marker when it runs on theirs

### Q2 · A degree-of-success upgrade on **your own** roll — where does the star go?
**Decides 75 feats here, 176 by Foundry's count.** Feats: 29, 30, 34, 38.

"If you roll a success on a Thievery check to Pick a Lock, you get a critical success instead." No
number moves. The app has no field for this at all — it currently lives as prose inside a situational
note, computed by nobody.

**(a)** a star on the skill (per Ruling C, every skill that could do it) · **(b)** a star on the
**action** it names, per Ruling D's Magic Hands shape · **(c)** both

Sub-question for #30/#34: when it applies to saves generally, does it star **all three** saves?

### Q3 · Battle forms — how much does the app compute?
**Decides 44 feats.** Feats: 18, 23.

A battle form **replaces** your statistics rather than adding to them: pest form *sets* your AC, *sets*
your attack modifier and damage, overrides your Speed and senses. Nothing in the app replaces a stat.

**(a)** a display-only mode carrying the printed text, computing nothing · **(b)** a mode that really
overrides the stats it names · **(c)** out of scope, the player reads the spell

### Q4 · Enemy-condition feats — star, or nothing?
Feats: 37 (and it reopens 6).

Cornered Fury and Mastermind's Eye have **identical payloads**: a foe becomes off-guard, nothing else.
For Mastermind's Eye you asked for a star on every Recall Knowledge skill — because that is where the
player looks it up. Cornered Fury is a **reaction with no skill** attached.

**(a)** the reaction entry is the whole surface · **(b)** it needs a star somewhere too — but where?

### Q5 · "You gain access to uncommon X" — a requirement at all?
**Decides 80 feats.** Feat: 22.

The lane manual's NOT-lanes rules out "access clauses", but that line was written about a record's own
*prerequisites*, not about a feat that **widens what you may buy or select**.

**(a)** no requirement · **(b)** the pickers must actually offer the newly-accessible items

## Narrower calls

### Q6 · Creature traits — recorded or not?
**16 feats.** Feat: 27. *"You gain the undead and zombie traits."* That changes what spells and effects
target you. **(a)** record them on the sheet · **(b)** flavour, the GM tracks it

### Q7 · A granted movement type's `*` — always, or only when the number can't speak?
Feat: 14. You asked for a `*` on Wyrmling Flight's fly Speed. Was that because **every** granted speed
should carry its source, or because *"equal to your Speed"* is a formula a bare number can't convey?

### Q8 · Does a granted activity's **interior** need modelling?
Feats: 24, 26. Overawe Crowd costs a Mythic Point and rolls Intimidation; Hydraulic Maneuvers gives a
later Athletics check a 15-foot range and suppresses item bonuses on it. Your Ruling D marked the Leap
action for Black Powder Boost — is that the shape here too, or is listing the action enough?

### Q9 · Constraining **another record's** choice list
Feats: 40, 41. Primal Dragonblood narrows which options another feat may pick; Domain Fluency should
offer only your mystery's domains but currently offers all 49. Principle C is about modifying a
*grant*, not filtering a *list*.

### Q10 · Weapon familiarity: replace the category rank, or floor it?
**9 feats.** Feat: 35. "For the purpose of proficiency, treat martial X as simple" — does the remapped
rank **replace** the weapon's normal rank, or do you take whichever is higher?

### Q11 · Where does a permanently-running spell live?
Feat: 31. Eternal Blessing runs Bless on you forever. You are always at the centre of your own
emanation, so unlike Ruling M's aura the self-facing half is unconditional: a real +1 status to your
attack rolls. Mode, passive effect, or star?

### Q12 · Explicit fields vs derived display
Feat: 10. When a feat grants an innate spell at its base rank once per day, must the record spell out
`rank` and `usesPerDay` when the sheet already derives and displays both? A style call with a
consistency cost either way.

### Q13 · Sense display when one supersedes another
Feat: 17. Nine feats grant darkvision to characters who already have low-light vision. Show **only**
darkvision, or both with the superseded one marked?

### Q14 · Picks recorded but not wired
Feat: 42. Improbable Elixirs records its picks in a free-text field with an explicit inert note. Should
they reach the Alchemy panel, or is recording them the intended end of it?

## Two things that are not questions for you

**#21 Divine Access is ambiguous in the data, not in the rules.** The slug names two different records
— the legacy APG feat and the remaster one — and they behave differently. That is a data-identity
problem for me to resolve, not a decision for you.

**#33 Natural Ambition** turns on when a pick is made during character creation, which is a builder
sequencing matter rather than a sheet requirement.

## Why nothing came back "confident"

I told the challenger to demote anything where two reasonable builders could disagree, and it demoted
all 19 it saw. That prior was too strong and the count is mine, not a finding.

But its reasoning was sound where I checked it, and it caught a real error: **Fortunate Blow says
"the next *creature other than you*", not "the next ally"** — an enemy who attacks that target
benefits too. Every reader had called it ally-facing. That is the kind of thing a gold set exists to
stop, and it argues for keeping the bar high rather than lowering it.
