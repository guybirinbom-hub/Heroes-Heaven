  bound, as wg-values.mjs:141-150. `conditional` is credited from a row whose level > 1, because their

  BLAST RADIUS MEASURED, and it is why a file-wide REGISTRY_KINDS entry for advancement.ts was REJECTED:
  a file-wide entry credits every key KEY_RE finds (~30 class and subclass keys) with a whole chassis
  each. Bounded to subclass-option ids, exactly THREE tables qualify — warpriest, battle-creed and
  light-mortar-innovation — and a class-keyed table ('cleric', 'druid') is credited to nobody.

TEACH 5 (plumbing) — scripts/wg-diff.mjs gained `--modes`, `--advancement` and `--derive`
  The anti-laundering hooks, the twins of `--core` (wg-values already shipped `--advancement`). A teach

  shipped table and reports nothing, which looks exactly like a teach that works. This is the same hole
  the first instrument pass found in wg-identity and closed with `--core`.
