FINDING: magical-knowledge#instrument  (CONFIRMED, internal)
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
What I did: one comparer TEACH in scripts/wg-identity.mjs — the lane-file option reader inside
`ourIdentities` (the `for (const f of ... featGrantsAuto/featGrants/featGrantsLane)` block, now at
:505-535). Two bugs, both confirmed by replaying the reader's own extraction:

  1. `/options\s*:\s*\[/` cannot match the JSON-quoted `"options": [` that every MACHINE-WRITTEN lane
     row uses — including featGrantsLane.ts:70, the magical-knowledge row. The key is optionally
     quoted now: `/['"]?options['"]?\s*:\s*\[([^\]]*)\]/g`.
  2. Their side labels an untitled ADJ_VALUE option from `operation.data.variable` (wg-identity.mjs:88),
     so SKILL_ARCANA keys as 'skillarcana' and could never meet a bare key('arcana'). Each lane option
     is now added under both spellings — `out.options.add(key(o))` and `out.options.add(key('skill '+o))`
     — exactly as the trainedSkillChoice reader at :415-416 already does for the identical reason.

  A third, smaller change in the same block: the lane-file list is read through
  `arg('--lane-files', <the same three paths>)` so a test can re-run the real comparer against a
  STUNTED copy of a lane file. Default behaviour is byte-identical.

  Print authority: AoN feat-4720 — *"Increase your proficiency rank in one of Arcana, Nature,
  Occultism, or Religion from expert to master and in another from trained to expert."* Our carrier
  (featGrantsLane.ts:70 skillChoices, two slots, four options each) already says exactly that, is
  rendered as two PopupSelects (Builder.tsx:2189-2230) and applied at build.ts:5706. Nothing about the
  RULE changed; only the comparer's ability to see it.
