parity batch 030 (WG-comparison lane): 40 records read, 33 findings fixed, 132 overlay rows, 5 code lanes

Cut 40 records at level <= 8 and read each one three ways (printed text, Wanderer's Guide's raw ops,
every carrier on our side), each read followed by an adversarial verifier: 33 findings CONFIRMED
(29 of them player-visible), 2 REFUTED, 0 for the owner. Six family specs applied 132 overlay rows
through scripts/data/effect-backfill.json — 127 new, 5 superseding — and every row was read back from
the shipped artefacts, with no earlier overlay row lost or narrowed against the batch-start commit
f0a6cb90. Verdicts derived by scripts/wg-batch-close.mjs: 27 FIXED, 13 MATCHES, 7 flagged residues.

WHAT THE ROWS FIXED. Four archetype "Breadth" feats aimed their extra spell slots at nothing or at the
wrong pool: arcane-breadth, apparition-magic and bloodline-breadth carried a spellSlotBonus with no
entryId, so slotEntryFor put the slots on the character's first CLASS entry (a fighter dipping the
archetype got none at all), and frozen-breadth carried no spellSlotBonus whatsoever. Each now names
its own archetype entry (wizard-dedication-casting, animist-dedication-casting,
sorcerer-dedication-casting, first-frost-casting). cantrip-supremacy gained the keepsake's 10 cantrips
on the ARCHETYPE entry, so the class cantrip cap stays where print leaves it. kaiju-stance's
Shattering Earth stored "fatal d12" in the space form that matches neither fatal reader, so the strike
showed no fatal die at all; it is now fatal-d12. Also: grasping-corpses, defensive-stratagem,
instinctive-maneuvers, transcribe-moment, selfless-parry, thoughtsense, sanguine-transference,
boost-summons, ilverani-purist, watchers-armband and dweomerveil, plus 109 specific-magic-armour rows
in the builtInRunes lane (each carrying {potency[, resilient]} re-derived from the record's own
printed name, so the armour delivers the fundamental runes it is named for).

WHAT THE CODE LANES ADDED. build.ts extraPickCount now runs the ikon group through applyCounterMods,
which is the first reader 'ikon-picks' has ever had — additional-ikon and second-ikon were authored
and dead, and the exemplar is now offered the fourth ikon that print grants. types.ts gained
critSpecRequiresFeat (a feat-held gate beside the existing mode-group one) and derive.ts honours it,
so viking-vindicator extends the viking weapon lists only for a character who holds the familiarity or
specialist feat. derive.ts deriveAc and resilientSaveBonus now floor on an armour's builtInRunes, so a
named "+2 resilient" armour is not one row of AC without its saves. attachments.ts accepts a
non-armour talisman host (affixHostAs) for bands-of-force. situationalBonuses.ts carries the four
permission-shaped clauses print states and no number expresses (improved-command-corpse,
eclectic-skill's untrained gate, deadly-butterfly, peer-beyond's initiative). MainTab.tsx widens the
stance-toggle source past character.feats, so the five stances with no feat record — arcane-cascade,
bullet-dancer, claw, talon and tenacious — finally get a chip; that is the whole of
unshaken-in-iron#stance, whose data was already right.

missing mechanic, and each was answered by teaching the instrument and pinning the teach mutation-
proof (the comparer must still report the record once the carrier is stunted): wg-diff now reads
untrainedProficiency and armorSpec, and reads the runes bucket through a kind map — {resilient:
['save']}, deliberately per-kind and not a blanket, so armor-potency and soaring keep reporting. That
teach also quiets resilient-greater and resilient-major, which are the same rune at higher tiers and
are dispositioned in work/.b030-gaps.json. wg-values compares the RuneDef's NUMBER rather than
crediting it. Two now-stale settles ('untrained-improvisation', 'armor-specialist') were deleted.
The closer added one settle: VERIFIED_EQUIVALENT['additional-ikon'] = ['choice'], because the fourth
ikon is a real pick whose carrier is two code paths (a COUNTER_MODS row and extraPickCount) rather
than a field on the record — the fighter-weapon-mastery precedent — pinned mutation-proof under
--raw in test/batch030-closer.test.ts. Three records were parked as EXPERIENCE instrument limits with
their hand verification recorded in work/experience-instrument-limits.json: frozen-breadth and
cantrip-supremacy deliver onto an archetype pool the harness's bare level-20 fighter host does not
have (both are pinned instead on fully built characters), and unshaken-in-iron's armour
specialization has no surface on a host that wears no armour.
