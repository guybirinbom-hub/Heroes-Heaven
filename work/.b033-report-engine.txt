armor-innovation#suit-item — BUILT (code).
  Where: src/rules/build.ts, the granted-items collection block, a new arm directly after the class-
  features arm (`content.classFeatures[fid]?.grantsItems`) and before the heritage arm. The innovation
  type is resolved with the SAME expression the `inventor` build block below already uses
  (ownsClass('inventor') ? innovationType(subclassOf('inventor')) : takenFeats.has('inventor-
  dedication') ? innovationType(choiceFlagAnswer('innovation', …)) : undefined), and the picked
  `build.inventorArmorStats` is pushed as { itemId, worn: true, source: 'Armor Innovation' }.
  Why here and not a data row: the item IS the answer to a per-character question, so no static
  `grantsItems` on classFeatures/armor-innovation can express it — power-suit for one character and
  subterfuge-suit for the next.
  Not defaulted: with no suit chosen nothing is granted. Print says "Choose one of the sets of
  statistics", and build.ts:1262 already reports "Armor base" as an incomplete build item; the
  power-suit default at build.ts:8362 exists only to filter modification options and is deliberately
  not reused as an inventory decision.
  Test: test/batch033-engine.test.ts, describe 'armor-innovation hands the chosen suit over as a worn
  item' — two its. The first asserts the item is present, `worn`, attributed to 'Armor Innovation',
  and that deriveAc is exactly +5 over the same character without it (i.e. it reaches the sheet, not
  just the pack). The second asserts the Subterfuge Suit when that is the pick, no suit at all when
  the question is unanswered, and no suit for a weapon-innovation inventor holding a stale answer.
  BLAST RADIUS: characters whose innovation is the ARMOR one and who have answered the Armor base
  question — a new worn armour appears in their inventory, so AC, Dex cap, armour check penalty,
  Speed penalty, the Strength requirement and Bulk all change for them. Nothing else: a weapon /
  construct / light-mortar inventor, an inventor who has not answered, and every non-inventor are
  untouched. Re-ran the 19 test files that build an inventor or name armor-innovation — all green.
