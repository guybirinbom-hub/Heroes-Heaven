mighty-bulwark#replacement-wording — CHANGED the entry's bonus from "+4 (bulwark)" to
  "+4 instead of your Dexterity modifier" and its `when` to "on all Reflex saves, damaging or not".
  The bulwark number REPLACES Dex (trait-549: "you add a +3 modifier instead of your Dexterity
  modifier") and feat-6413 only raises it, so the old string read as a flat +4 — for a high-Dex
  character an overstatement. explain.ts's featSituationalLines passes `bonus` verbatim into the
  popup, so the string is what the player reads; there is no number to correct because the app cannot
  know whether an incoming effect is a damaging one.
  TEST: describe "mighty-bulwark reads as a replacement for Dexterity, once", first case — a fighter
  in Hellknight Plate reads "instead of your Dexterity modifier" and never "+4 (bulwark)".
