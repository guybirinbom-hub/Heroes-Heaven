ITEM 1 — src/rules/explain.ts: the Speed popup blamed armour for a bonus the type rule suppressed
  BUILT. Raised as CROSS-FILE GAPS 1 of both work/.b037-report-red-owner-ruled-engine-lanes.txt and
  work/.b037-verify-red-owner-ruled-engine-lanes.txt, and correctly left unbuilt there (explain.ts was
  another group's file that round).

  THE REGRESSION, re-measured here and not taken on the report's word: deriveSpeeds learned this batch
  to take the HIGHEST of two bonuses sharing a type (swashbucklers-speed#typed-all-speeds, feat-6238
  *"You gain a +5-foot status bonus to your Speeds"*). The `case 'speed'` block of explain.ts kept
  SUMMING every carrier's landSpeedBonus itself, and then reported the difference against deriveSpeeds
  as a penalty. A character holding Swashbuckler's Speed (+5 status) and Scout's Speed (+10 status),
  wearing no armour at all, read: Ancestry Speed 25 · Speed increase 5 · Speed increase 10 · "Armor
  Speed penalty — heavy armor or unmet Strength" −5, under a correct total of 35 ft. The number was
  right; the popup named a cause that did not exist.

  THE FIX, and why it is that shape: the block's whole purpose is that the parts SUM to the header
  (test/speed-increases.test.ts "the Speed breakdown sums to the Speed"), so the stacking rule has to
  be mirrored here rather than the difference re-labelled. `landBonusOf` is hoisted out of the loop,
  the winning carrier per named type is found once (highest, first-wins on a tie — the same order
  deriveSpeeds settles), and a losing typed bonus now contributes 0 and keeps a VISIBLE line reading
  "<record> — status bonus, does not stack with <winner>". Nothing is dropped, the untyped lane is
  untouched (Fleet still stacks and still shows its own +5), and the surplus/deficit reporting either
  side of the loop is unchanged for every other case.

  Pinned by: test/batch037-closer-red.test.ts, describe "swashbucklers-speed: the Speed breakdown
  agrees with the Speed it suppresses" — four cases, each cited
  `// batch 037: swashbucklers-speed#typed-all-speeds` with the record id in the describe title:
    · the pair pays the printed 35 feet once;
    · no "Armor Speed penalty" part on an unarmoured character;
    · the parts still sum to 35 and the suppressed line names Scout's Speed at value 0;
    · Fleet, untyped, still stacks to 40 and still shows its own part.
  Every assertion runs on an IN-MEMORY patched copy of the database, so none of them flips when a
  driver applies a row.
