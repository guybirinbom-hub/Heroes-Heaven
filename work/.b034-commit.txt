parity batch 034 (WG-comparison lane): 41 records, 51 overlay rows, five code lanes

Forty records cut at level <= 9, read by an Opus reader plus an adversarial verifier: 42
confirmed findings, 4 refuted, 2 for the owner. Seven family specs applied through the driver
(situational, data-rows, instruments-1, instruments-2, engine, gap-data-rows, gap-engine): 57
rows offered, scripts/data/effect-backfill.json 14158 -> 14209, 2 declared supersessions, and 19
records created that did not exist before (18 construct modifications and the Drain Familiar
action) with their verbatim printed prose.

WHAT THE PLAYER GETS
- Construct Innovation is a real picker: the label-only `choice` row is retired and 18 construct
  modification records (7 initial, 6 breakthrough, 5 revolutionary) are created with the printed
  text from innovation-2, so the inventor's three tier selects offer them like every other
  innovation type.
- A Silent Whisper psychic gets Sending as their 6th-rank granted spell instead of a second 5th:
  the conscious-mind ladder now carries a `rank` and build.ts files the grant under it.
- Improved Familiar Attunement grants Drain Familiar (a new action record with arcane-thesis-7's
  own words) AND retires Drain Bonded Item through a new `replacesActions` lane in MainTab.
- Elemental Blast shows its variable action cost; Way of the Triggerbrand states its printed
  triggerbrand access; the Pistolero's advanced deed is retagged to the remastered printing
  (Pistoler's Retort, action-912, edition remaster), which is what actually delivers it.
- Beast and Psychopomp eidolons show their printed Language line (Sylvan, Requian); the familiar
  budget of Improved Familiar Attunement is 3 with growth at 6/12/18.
- Six new situational riders reach the sheet: The Silent Whisper's daze range and message reach,
  Elemental Instinct's rage concealment, Victor's Wreath on spell attacks, Scar of the Survivor's
  whole Immanence sentence (Diehard included, now gated as print gates it), and Curse of Turbulent
  Moments; the duplicated Segmented Frame Stealth star is gone.

WHAT WAS SETTLED, AND WHY
Twelve comparer misreads were taught rather than patched: an option's `grant.whileActive` and a
subclass option's `keyAbility` are now visible to wg-diff (which is why dragon-instinct,
superstition-instinct, gathered-lore and wandering-reverie went quiet outside the batch - each
cited in work/.b034-gaps.json with the carrier dumped and verified). Five druid orders
(animal, leaf, storm, untamed, wave) settle `specialStat` alone: their createValue
MAIN_DRUID_ORDER is our subclass pick, the same decision batch 033 recorded for flame, spore and
stone, one entry per order and cultivation-order still reporting as the control. Weapon
Innovation credits its off-record tier pickers, where the level gate and the choice are one
mechanism. Every settle carries a mutation-proof test.

QUEUED FOR THE OWNER (work/owner-questions.json)
#139 spore-order (re-key of #134), #140 scar-of-the-survivor, #141 skybearer's-belt,
#142 echo-of-lost-moments, #143 curse-of-turbulent-moments. Three are WG-vs-print conflicts where
we match print; two are engine-shape questions - what carries a Diehard-style dying threshold
under an immanence toggle, and what carries a mode-gated scaling penalty (WG hangs -1..-4 off the
four cursebound modes; ours are class-scoped with no mystery discriminator).

DEFERRED
The construct-companion applier for c.inventor.modifications (six of the eighteen modifications
carry companion mechanics and only two have a field to land in; WG models none of them either) -
parked as flagged residue, reaching the player as the printed prose in the meantime. The
name-collision precedence rule between an action record and a class feature of the same name is
still unwritten; this batch fixed the one instance.

SUITE TRIAGE
Three batch-034 tests were written as shipped-vs-patched deltas and flipped the moment the driver
applied their rows; each is re-anchored on a stunted copy so it cannot flip again (the
replacesActions control now DELETES the field, and construct-innovation's "no records shipped" leg
reproduces the defect by stripping the tag). One inherited red: the umbrella ratchet was 440 and
the shipped items give 441 - measured against the commit that last set the constant, `throneglass`
(equipment-5212, unpriced, above two priced grade blocks) is the only id that joined and nothing
left, so it is new content and the ratchet rises by one. All nine gates, the full suite (5680) and
the verify chain are green; the three checks still red were red before this batch started.
