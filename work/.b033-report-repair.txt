lurker-in-devouring-dark#avatar-damage-type  — CONFIRMED, repaired

WHAT THE FINDING IS. AoN apparition-7 prints the avatar Strike as "**Damage** 6d10+6 bludgeoning plus
Grab"; we ship "**Damage** 6d10+6 plus Grab". The @Damage cleaner kept the dice and ate the TYPE, so
the player reading the avatar's melee attack has the number but not the half of a Strike that
resistances, weaknesses and immunities are read against. Nothing looks broken, which is why it
shipped.

WHAT I BUILT.

  scripts/repair-damage-type.mjs (new) — `restoreDamageTypes(ours, theirs)` and
  `findDroppedDamageType(root)`. Insertions only: the printed type word is spliced in immediately
  after the dice run we already print, and every other byte of our description — our ⟨1⟩ glyphs, our
  de-linked "Grab", our blank lines, any authored correction — is left alone. It never replaces our
  prose with the mirror's. Four refusals, because a confident wrong damage type is worse than a
  visible missing one:
    1. ORDERED ALIGNMENT. Sites are paired BY ORDER and the record is refused unless the counts match
       and every pair's dice run is identical. This is load-bearing, not belt-and-braces: Crafter in
       the Vault rolls 6d6+6 slashing in melee and 6d6+6 piercing at range, so a dice-string lookup
       would have handed one of them the other's type.
    2. TAIL AGREEMENT. The first word after the site must agree on both sides ("plus", "Ranged", or
       end-of-block).
    3. VOCABULARY. Only a PF2e damage type (with optional persistent/splash qualifier) is insertable.
    4. ALREADY CORRECT / PRINT WINS. A site that already carries its type is skipped (re-running is a
       no-op), and where AoN itself prints no type — Devil Form's vordine hoof, "1d4+12 plus 1d6
       fire", spell-894 — nothing is invented.
  It reuses `MIRROR`, `plain` and `mirrorIndex` from scripts/repair-stripped-save-dc.mjs rather than
  re-deriving them, and follows that script's CLI shape (report by default, `--write` emits the spec;
  I never ran --write — the spec below was generated from the same exported function and the driver
  applies it).
