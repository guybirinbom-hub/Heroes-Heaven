1. golden-league-xun-dedication#already-expert — ENGINE HALF BUILT, data half cross-file.
   Print (AoN feat-2734): "You gain expert proficiency in two of the following skills (or in two other
   skills of your choice in which you're trained, if you were already an expert in the listed skills)".
   · src/rules/build.ts, the skillChoices redundancy block (~5580): the replacement now lands at
     `at(grant)` — the rank the dead slot itself would have given — instead of the hard-coded 'trained',
     and the fallback entry carries that rank. Above trained the pick is also gated on the character
     ALREADY BEING TRAINED in it, which is the half of the parenthetical nothing said before.
   · src/rules/types.ts — Character.skillFallbacks entries gain `rank?: ProficiencyRank`, present only
     when the replacement is worth more than training.
   · src/builder/Builder.tsx, the replacement picker: heading reads the rank actually held ("Already
     expert in Athletics", not "Already trained"), and the dead-option predicate now greys a skill the
     character is already at the fallback rank in AND (above trained) a skill they are untrained in,
     each with its own reason.
   · Tests: 'golden-league-xun-dedication reports its dead slots as expert-rank replacements',
     '…raises a replacement the character is TRAINED in to expert', '…gives an UNTRAINED replacement
     nothing', plus the no-regression control '…angelkin, unchanged'.
   · COULD NOT DO: the two `redundantFallback` flags (featGrantsAuto.ts) — cross-file, listed above.
   · BLAST RADIUS of the rank-aware replacement, measured over all three grant registries: exactly ONE
     shipped record has a redundantFallback-reachable skillChoices slot whose grant exceeds 'trained' —
     lion-blade-dedication (conditionalRank upgraded to expert). A holder already EXPERT in the chosen
     Deception/Stealth now gets an expert replacement instead of a trained one, and must pick a skill
     they are already trained in. Print gives that character nothing at all either way, so this is an
     existing over-grant changing shape, not a new one; the cross-file line above is the real fix.
     Every other record's slot grants 'trained', where `at(grant)` === 'trained' and nothing moves.

3. basic-modification — ENGINE BUILT, no row needed.
   Print (AoN feat-3117): "You gain a basic modification of your choice for your innovation."
   Rather than the proposal's second 28-option `choice` picker (which would duplicate the tiered
   modification picker the app already has, and needs `ownsFeature` plumbing on the dedication), the
   EXISTING lane was widened to the archetype:
   · src/rules/build.ts, the inventor block (~8072): `inventorViaDedication` resolves the innovation
     type from Inventor Dedication's own answer via `choiceFlagAnswer('innovation', …)` — its values are
     the same subclass ids `innovationType` already maps — mirroring `runesmithViaDedication` beside it.
     `validPick` closes the breakthrough/revolutionary tiers to a dedicated character and opens the
     initial tier only while they hold basic-modification.
   · src/builder/shared.tsx, the modifications block: same gate, so the control the widening needs
     actually renders for an archetype inventor (and only the Initial row does).
   · Tests: 'basic-modification resolves the archetype innovation and keeps the initial pick', 'the
     modification … becomes an OWNED class feature', 'a dedicated inventor WITHOUT basic-modification
     keeps no modification', '…the later tiers stay closed to the archetype at 20'.
   · BLAST RADIUS: `Character.inventor` is now populated for a dedicated inventor. Its four readers were
     checked — derive.ts fullLevelResistanceTarget (gated on a class-only resistanceLevelUpgrade feature,
     unaffected), derive.ts ownedFeatureIds (now includes the chosen modification: intended — it is what
     makes its situational bonuses and modes fire), FeatsTab (lists the modification: intended), and
     buildFromCharacter's `dcOwns('inventor')` round-trip (restores the pick: intended). A dedicated
     inventor with no modification picked gets `modifications: {}` and nothing else changes.
