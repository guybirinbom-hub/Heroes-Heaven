SCOPE AND METHOD

159 records. 21 lanes cover 146 of them; 11 records are singletons that no lane reaches; 2 are wholly impossible. Ten more records appear in BOTH a lane and the singleton or impossible bucket, because one printed half is buildable and the other is not — those are marked PARTIAL rather than silently rounded up.

Two of the four cluster proposals arrived truncated, so lanes for those quarters (g2 and g3 — 79 records) were rebuilt from the records themselves in work/lanes/all159.json rather than taken on trust. Every structural claim below was checked against the source before a lane was kept.

WHAT I DROPPED, AND WHY

Several clusters proposed lanes for things the engine already has. All were verified and demoted to DATA, which is why lane 1 exists and ranks first:
  - shadow-spells / forgotten-presence were flagged "there is no spell-list-addition lane". There is: spellListAdditions (types.ts:814), read at build.ts:2659-2667, consumed by SpellsTab.tsx:246-247. What is actually missing is only the array/feature/repertoire widening, which is lane 5.
  - insulated-poppet was flagged "every IWR field is additive". removesWeaknesses (types.ts:854) is read at derive.ts:928 and insulated-poppet is a FEAT, so the field reaches it today. Pure data.
  - astral / impactful and their greater versions were flagged as needing a rune-payload lane. RuneDef.damage exists (types.ts:1762) and flaming ships it; core.json simply omits the key on these four. Pure data.
  - The five mythic callings were flagged "unmodelled". Nine *-calling rows already ship in FEAT_SITUATIONAL (situationalBonuses.ts:2861-2952) in exactly the drafted shape, and derive.ts:1192 already puts c.mythicCalling into ownedFeatureIds. A registry fill, costed as data.
  - miraculous-flight's own cluster said it was a registry fill, and it is: COMPANION_MODS already carries speeds:{fly:'land'}.
  - blazons-of-shared-power was flagged as needing a rune-copy lane. copyRunesFrom exists (types.ts:1941) with a full applier at derive.ts:1104 and a manual picker in InventoryTab.tsx:1028. Only the AUTOMATIC trigger is hardcoded, as DOUBLING_RINGS at derive.ts:1083 — a two-id Set. Folded into lane 4.
  - masterful-hunter-precision's whyFlagged says "the base Precision edge is equally unmodelled". It is modelled: derive.ts:1266-1272 emits the 1d8/2d8/3d8 annotation gated on the precision subclass and the Hunt Prey toggle. Only the Nth-hit extension is missing, and only as text.

THE SINGLE BIGGEST STRUCTURAL FINDING

The engine has a systematic hole that no cluster stated as one thing: a dozen appliers read a field off a FEAT and nothing ever looks the same field up on an owned CLASS FEATURE. Verified sites — derive.ts:310 untrainedProficiency, :928 removesWeaknesses, :1227 choice.ownsFeature, :1423 weaponTraits, :1807 unarmedTraits, :2086 bulkLimitBonus; build.ts:2289/2403/2705/3699/3761 FEAT_GRANTS, :2661 spellListAdditions, :3272 the languages IIFE.

The proof it is a hole and not a design decision: featGrantsAuto.ts:132 ships 'expert-overdrive': { skills: { crafting: 'expert' } } and :197 ships 'legendary-overdrive': { skills: { crafting: 'legendary' } }. Both keys are classFeature ids. Every FEAT_GRANTS lookup passes a featId. Neither has ever fired. Someone already authored the data for a lane the engine cannot read. Lane 2 resurrects it, and lanes 3, 4 and 5 cannot land at all until it does — the inventor's 19 innovation modifications, the seven runelord sins and the wizard curriculum lists are ALL classFeatures.

THE SECOND STRUCTURAL FINDING

The app never links a class choice to an inventory item. InventorBuild (types.ts:916) stores an innovation TYPE and three modification ids and no item; the thaumaturge's weapon implement and the wizard's bonded weapon are not recorded anywhere at all. That single absence kills 19 inventor modifications, both crit-spec clauses, the armour restat, the rune cap and Rod of Rule. It is lane 4, and it is why lane 3's inventor half cannot ship alone.

THIRD: ownsClass() IS A WALL

Verified gates in build.ts at :1362, :2022, :2261, :2623, :3110, :3134, :3154, :3175. An archetype taker gets none of the class machinery even when the archetype ALREADY RECORDED THE PICK — thaumaturge-dedication stores dedicationImplement, barbarian-dedication stores barbarianInstinct, order-training stores the order. Meanwhile ClassResource.feat (classResources.ts:29-31) exists for exactly this purpose and already ships for Rage, Panache and Ranger. Lane 7 finishes a job the codebase started.

RANKING RULE APPLIED

Rank = (records covered) x (confidence it reaches the sheet), cheapest first among equals. That puts lane 1 (16 records, zero engine change, every field already read) first and lane 2 (6 direct records but a hard prerequisite for ~40 more, one afternoon of plumbing) second, ahead of lane 3, which covers the most records (29) but is the largest single piece of work and half-blocked on lane 4.

THIN LANES, DECLARED

Lanes 15, 16, 20 and 21 cover two records each, and lane 18 covers one. They are kept because in each case the missing CONCEPT — additive speed arithmetic, an armour rune with any payload, adjusting a mode you already have, a second heritage, a level-gated subclass grant — has no expression at all and the alternative is a hardcoded special case. Lane 18 is one record here and eighteen in the family (6 gunslinger ways x initial/advanced/greater), and the widened field is what every future subclass-grants-a-later-feature case needs. If a batch-count floor is imposed, these five are the ones that fall.

LANES WITH A SMALL SLICE HERE AND A LARGE FAMILY BEHIND THEM

Lane 12 is 4 records here, 13 apparitions and ~485 deities in the family. Lane 3's inventor half is 7 here and 19 in the family. Lane 9's daily-choice half is 1 here (skin-hard-as-horn) and ~10 in the family, with the siblings named: pelt-of-the-beast already carries a daily choice and grants nothing, and planar-resilience, environmental-adaptability, demon-hunter, the-queen-mother, viridian-crown and endless-memories all record an answer today and apply nothing. Sixty-seven records in total carry a FeatChoiceDef.daily whose answer the Rest sheet collects and discards, because a FeatChoiceDef option is {value,label,description} and carries no grant.

THE HONEST CEILINGS

"Attempt the check at mythic proficiency" renders as a PHRASE, not a number, in all fourteen calling entries — PROFICIENCY_RANKS stops at legendary. Making it compute is a rules-content decision about the proficiency ladder that would retroactively give a number to ~20 shipped entries; it is not a gap and is deliberately not in this plan.

Every "first time each day you critically succeed" clause is a reminder. The app never sees a roll.

Lane 3 is where over-granting is most dangerous: an unfiltered weaponTraits rider applies to every weapon the character wields, and an unfiltered critSpec grant lights up every Strike (derive.ts:1347 weaponMatches returns true for an unnarrowed entry). In both cases the FILTER and the DATA must ship in the same change, never separately.

BUILD ORDER, NOT JUST RANK

Lane 2 is a hard dependency of lanes 3, 4 and 5. Lane 4 is a hard dependency of the inventor half of lane 3 and of the two heavy-construction / rune-capacity singletons. Lane 1 depends on nothing and blocks nothing, so it can be done in parallel by whoever is touching data rather than code. Lane 16's actsAs arithmetic and the blessed-shield singleton share a max-not-sum helper and should be written once.
