  Print: *"You gain a skill feat associated with each of the skills you chose."* The feat chooses TWO
  skills, so it grants TWO skill feats. WG encodes ONE FILTERED select; print is the authority, so the
  count is 2 and WG's single select is their engine's shape, not a ruling.

  src/rules/featGrants.ts — `FeatGrant.bonusSkillFeat` widened `boolean` → `boolean | number`, plus two
  exported helpers beside the other FEAT_GRANTS readers:
    · `bonusSkillFeatCount(g)` — a number is the count, the legacy `true` is exactly 1, absent is 0.
    · `bonusSkillFeatKey(featId, i)` — index 0 is the BARE feat id, index 1+ is `<featId>:<i>`. That is
      the convention the pick-a-feat and loreChoices slots already use for their second and later
      picks, and it is why no character saved before this batch moves: its one answer is still index 0.
  src/rules/featGrantsLane.ts — 'magical-knowledge' gains `"bonusSkillFeat": 2` (the printed clause is
    quoted in the comment above the entry). Its two skillChoices slots are untouched.
  src/rules/build.ts — the injection loop (the "Dedication BONUS skill feats" block) walks
    bonusSkillFeatCount and reads bonusSkillFeatKey per index instead of the bare feat id; the
    reconstruction loop in deriveBuildFromCharacter now looks for the first FREE INDEX on a same-level
    grant rather than for an untouched feat id, so reopening a Magical Knowledge character keeps both
    picks instead of dropping the second into a real skill-feat slot. BuildState.dedicationSkillFeats'
    doc comment records the key shape.
  src/builder/Builder.tsx — one "Bonus skill feat" SubCard per granted feat, keyed by
    bonusSkillFeatKey. Each picker hides the answers of its siblings, because buildCharacter drops a
    duplicate feat and a printed grant must not be spendable on nothing.

  Tests (test/batch032-gap-engine.test.ts, describe title carries the record id):

      pinning that the legacy carrier did not silently widen.
    · "both bonus feats are recovered onto their own keys, not one" — deriveBuildFromCharacter round trip.

  FEAT_LANE_GRANTS after this change: rogue-dedication (`true`, unchanged in every path — count 1, bare
