WHY THESE ARE RESIDUE AND NOT AN OWNER QUESTION

I re-ran the carrier sweep rather than taking the builder's word for it, and it holds in both directions:

- Character side: every land-Speed lane targets the CHARACTER. derive.ts:5222-5224 sums landSpeedBonus
  over feats, ownedFeatureIds and heritages unconditionally; speeds / speedsIf / landSpeedMin /
  speedAdjust (derive.ts:5256, 5296) are all character-scoped. Nothing routes a character's class
  feature to a companion.
- Companion side: a companion land Speed DOES exist — src/rules/companions.ts:404,
  `speeds.land += m.speedBonus + (spec?.speedBonus ?? 0) + gear.speedBonus`. Its addends are exactly
  three and all companion-side: maturity, specialization, invested companion gear (companions.ts:283).
  There is no character-feature addend. And CompanionMod.speeds (companions.ts:531-536) is a Math.max
  SET of extra speed TYPES, not an addend, so even the existing companionGrants lane cannot express
  "+5 feet" — a redirect could not be smuggled in as a grant.
- The condition has no carrier either: a scan of every "mounted" occurrence in src/rules and src/sheet
  returns only situational STARS (situationalBonuses.ts:2424, 2426, 2438, 2547, 3309, 3465 — all of the
  form when: "while mounted…") and prose notes. There is no mounted state, mode or condition anywhere,
  so an engine redirect would first need a player-facing mounted toggle before it had anything to key on.

Scale, measured over public/core-descriptions.json (8 records mention a mount gaining something):
  - 5 are ordinary companion grants already carried by src/rules/companionGrants.ts —
    feats/faithful-steed, feats/auspicious-mount, feats/specialized-mount, feats/behold-a-pale-horse,
    feats/night-terror.
  - 3 are the SAME single Blessed Swiftness sentence seen from three records — classes/champion (class
    blurb), classFeatures/blessing-of-the-devoted (parent), classFeatures/blessed-swiftness (the record).
So the "redirect a bonus that would otherwise be yours to your mount" mechanic is ONE clause in the game.

Precedent decides the shape without an owner ruling: src/rules/situationalBonuses.ts:4358, swift-paragon,
carries the identical construction — *"If you're mounted on your animal companion at the start of your
turn, you can have the mount be quickened that turn instead of you."* — as a disclosure note on a
condition marker, not as a mechanical redirect. work/.b036-rows-data-rows-1.json does exactly that for
blessed-swiftness (the modeAdjust parenthetical now states the redirect instead of denying it), so the
player-visible half of this finding ships in this batch and the record is consistent with the one other
record in the game that has the same problem.

No owner question is owed on the three tests: print settles the mechanic unambiguously ("If you're
mounted, your mount gains the bonus instead."), WG does not contradict print — it encodes strictly less
(bare addBonusToValue SPEED 5 status, no mounted clause) — and no ruling of the owner's conflicts with
another. What is missing is engine capacity, not a decision, and building a mounted mode plus a
character→companion speed addend for a single clause is speculative infrastructure. If a second clause
of this shape ever lands, it wants its own measured lane with the mounted-state model decided up front.

Checked while I was in the records, so it is not left as an unknown: there is no double-count to fix.
I dumped the sibling features — only classFeatures/blessed-swiftness carries landSpeedBonus (5);
blessing-of-the-devoted, blessed-shield and blessed-armament carry none — so the character is not being
given +10, and the builder's decision to leave landSpeedBonus:5 in place is the correct on-foot value.
