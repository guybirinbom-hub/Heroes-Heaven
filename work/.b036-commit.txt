THE ONE SUITE RED, AND ITS TRIAGE. test/inventor-innovation.test.ts, "it upgrades ONLY the initial
modification, not every resistance the inventor has", built an inventor holding physical-protections in
the revolutionary slot with Enhanced Resistance in the breakthrough slot. Batch 036 enforced the printed
prerequisite on every revolutionary modification that carries one, and physical-protections' own is a
BREAKTHROUGH modification — innovation-5: "You must have the dense plating, layered mesh, or tensile
absorption breakthrough modification to select this modification." — which that build cannot hold,
because there is exactly one breakthrough slot and Enhanced Resistance is in it. So the old build became
illegal by print and the modification correctly granted nothing: the gate working, not a regression. The
case is re-aimed at energy-barrier, the same shape one modification over, whose prerequisite list is
initial-tier and is already satisfied by the build's phlogistonic-regulator. What the case is FOR — that
Enhanced Resistance rewrites the initial modification's formula and leaves every other modification's
alone — is unchanged, and now reads on six non-overlapping energy types instead of three physical ones.
Both premises are quoted verbatim from innovation-5 in the citation comments, and
scripts/test-flip-audit.mjs passes.
